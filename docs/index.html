Melody: the shape of your pitch contour in cents (median-centered, so singing in another key still scores), aligned by DTW. Timbre: an MFCC voice-fingerprint matched with DTW. Energy: the loud/quiet shape. Rhythm: when you paused and where you burst. Guardrails: a load-bearing axis below 15 caps the total at 45, and silence always scores 0.
